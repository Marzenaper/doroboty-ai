# Twój projekt

O3: rekruter potrzebuje zrozumieć wkład kandydata, umiejętności, kontekst AI, decyzje i problemy. F1/F3 oraz C2/C4 wskazują brak rozróżnienia własnej pracy, wsparcia zespołu i kontekstu projektu. I2/E2: rozmowa przed wysłaniem zgłoszenia, w istniejącym formularzu po CV.

Acceptance: Anna answers three prompts, edits and approves a project description, saves and resumes the draft, and submits with the existing consent. The approved description appears on both application records. The conversation and approval are required before the remaining form appears. Responses come from the candidate by typing or a browser microphone recording. Prepared prompts and deterministic composition simulate AI. Recordings are saved and replayed locally, without transcription.

Current test: text answers only. One optional follow-up on the first answer when rule-based cues suggest collective work without a personal action. Compare recruiter comprehension before and after. Previously saved recordings remain playable.
